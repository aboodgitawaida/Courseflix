# CourseFlix

A "Netflix-style" course recommendation and discovery platform built for University of Toronto students. 

Finding the right university courses shouldn't mean digging through Reddit threads and RateMyProf separately. CourseFlix brings everything together into a clean, personalized recommendation engine based on your major, past classes, and aggregated student reviewsss.

**Live App**: [courseflix.ca](https://www.courseflix.ca)
